# Super Economy and Bad Juju Evaluation

**Added:** v2.5, July 25, 2026  
**Purpose:** Correct the former bootstrap wording that implied Bad Juju was a presumptive top Super-generation weapon.

## 1. Correction

Bad Juju is a valid, unique Super-economy option. It is not automatically best in slot.

Official item behavior states that String of Curses:

- Refills the magazine on kills.
- Increases damage for a short duration.
- Grants Super energy based on the strength of String of Curses.

Official definition: <https://www.bungie.net/en/Explore/Detail/DestinyInventoryItemDefinition/2816212794>

## 2. System-output model

Total practical Super generation can come from:

- Direct perk-generated Super energy.
- Damage-based Super generation.
- Final-blow rate and enemy density.
- Orbs of Power and Siphon mods.
- Subclass fragments/aspects and elemental pickups.
- Exotic armor refunds or scalars.
- Armor-set bonuses and current mods.
- The current Super stat and sandbox rules.
- Activity modifiers.

The best Super-economy weapon is therefore the weapon that produces the strongest **whole-build result**, not necessarily the weapon whose description explicitly mentions Super energy.

## 3. Bad Juju strengths

- Primary-ammo simplicity.
- Magazine refill on kills.
- Damage ramp and direct Super contribution.
- Good fit when enemy density and durability allow String of Curses to remain active.
- Unique role worth retaining unless Brent deliberately abandons it.

## 4. Bad Juju limitations

- Requires kills and a maintainable chain.
- Can lose value when enemies are durable or sparse.
- Occupies the Exotic weapon slot.
- May provide less total Super and less activity utility than a stronger add-clear, orb-producing, Champion, or build-synergy weapon.
- Current behavior may be affected by sandbox changes and must be reverified before a definitive ranking.

## 5. Permanent vault wording

Use:

> Select the high-kill-efficiency, orb-producing, or direct-Super-generation weapon that best completes the build. Bad Juju is one option, not a presumptive BIS choice.

Do not use:

> Bad Juju or another strong Super-generation weapon.

## 6. Deletion/retention rule

- Retain one Bad Juju as a unique Exotic option unless Brent explicitly chooses otherwise.
- Do not protect multiple ordinary weapons simply because they have theoretical Super synergy.
- Compare total build output, activity role, Champion coverage, kill speed, orb production, and Exotic-slot opportunity cost.
- A stronger killing weapon can be the better Super weapon when the rest of the build rewards damage, final blows, or Orbs.
