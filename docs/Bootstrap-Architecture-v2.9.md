# Bootstrap and Reasoning Architecture — v2.9

## Purpose

v2.9 preserves the two-bootstrap and reasoning-control design while adding a dedicated Project North Star so purpose cannot be confused with process.

## Active layers

1. `Brents-Destiny-2-AI-Bootstrap.md` — full operating context and detailed rules.
2. `Brents-Destiny-2-Human-Bootstrap.md` — readable project story.
3. `docs/Project-North-Star.md` — mission, success metrics, and build-first priorities.
4. `docs/Brent-Decision-Doctrine.md` — reasoning depth, evidence handling, red-team tests, and communication.
5. `docs/Brent-Personal-Evidence-Ledger.md` — Brent-specific active truth and provenance.
6. `docs/State-Reconciliation-Protocol.md` — update transaction and release gates.
7. Newest DIM export — current inventory state.
8. Mechanics/policy/research references — task-specific depth.

## Why the North Star is separate

A doctrine can explain how to reason perfectly while optimizing the wrong objective. The North Star answers **why the project exists and what success means**:

- Primary: stronger and more enjoyable builds.
- Secondary: clarity and reduced decision fatigue.
- Tertiary: vault space.

This separation prevents future AI sessions from drifting back into maximum-retention or slot-count optimization merely because the repository contains extensive deletion safeguards.

## Required analysis flow

1. Define target content and build engine.
2. Reconcile Brent evidence and current inventory.
3. Map required weapon functions and best owned options.
4. Compare candidates with full mechanical safeguards.
5. Ask what stronger loadout keeping/deleting enables.
6. Classify Keep / Review / Delete.
7. Separately classify Chase / Craft / Focus priority.
8. Produce decision receipts and an Armory Improvement Plan.

The exact v2.8 AI bootstrap, human bootstrap, and Decision Doctrine are preserved under `docs/history/`. Active stable filenames are overwritten in place in future releases.
