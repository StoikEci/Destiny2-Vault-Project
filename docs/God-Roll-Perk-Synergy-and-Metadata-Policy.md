# God-Roll, Perk-Synergy, and Metadata Policy

## Core rule

Prioritize strong community-supported and mechanically coherent rolls. Do not preserve a poor roll merely because it is unique.

## Evidence order

1. Current Community Insights for exact mechanics.
2. Current community god-roll recommendations.
3. Perk-to-perk synergy.
4. Exact frame and archetype-specific stats.
5. Element, Champion, ammo, slot, range, and build role.
6. Origin trait, Tier flexibility, source, and reacquisition cost.
7. Positive personal evidence.

## Metadata

Positive only:

- Favorite
- Keep
- Lock
- Loadout
- Crafted
- Holofoil
- Kill history
- User note

Neutral:

- Zero kills
- Unlocked
- Untagged
- No loadout
- New or untested

## Safety

- Wishlist absence does not mean bad.
- Never delete the final Eager Edge sword.
- Do not collapse different frames or meaningful draw/charge-time packages.
- Every junk candidate needs an identified retained replacement and role explanation.
