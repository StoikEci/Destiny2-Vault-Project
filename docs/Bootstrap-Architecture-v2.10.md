## v2.10 armory logic

The prioritization order now leads with:

1. Build-engine enablers and bottleneck solvers.
2. Best-fit performance upgrades.
3. Exceptional fun and confidence.
4. High-leverage flexibility.
5. Credible loadout and encounter coverage.
6. Unique or difficult-to-replace capability.
7. Experimental potential with a concrete plan.
8. Redundant or outclassed inventory.

Deletion uses two gates:

- **Build-contribution gate:** Does the item materially improve power, fun, reliability, flexibility, or access to desired content?
- **Safeguard gate:** Would deletion lose a meaningful exact frame, firing behavior, element, origin trait, legal Tier configuration, costly-source capability, personal preference, metadata signal, or difficult-to-reacquire function?

This prevents two opposite failures: museum-style retention of technically different but unused items, and careless deletion that ignores real mechanical or personal capability.
