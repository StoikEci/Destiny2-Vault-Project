# Brent's Destiny 2 Armory and Build Intelligence Project v2.10

This repository packages Brent's Destiny 2 build-and-armory intelligence framework, current vault snapshots and analyses, reusable prompts, preserved research history, and a reasoning-control system designed to maximize build power and enjoyment while preventing stale or invented personal context.

## Choose the right starting file

### For an AI continuing or performing vault analysis

Read in this order:

1. `Brents-Destiny-2-AI-Bootstrap.md`
2. `docs/Project-North-Star.md`
3. `docs/Brent-Decision-Doctrine.md`
4. `docs/Brent-Personal-Evidence-Ledger.md`
5. The newest DIM export
6. Only the policy or research references needed for the task

The AI bootstrap explains the project and operating rules. The Project North Star defines the goal hierarchy: stronger and more enjoyable builds first, clarity second, space third. The Decision Doctrine explains how much and how deeply to think. The Personal Evidence Ledger records what Brent actually said, what is active versus historical, and what remains unknown.

### For a friend or other human

Open:

`Brents-Destiny-2-Human-Bootstrap.md`

It tells the story of the project, including why Destiny vault cleanup is difficult, how Tier 5 weapons and player fit complicate decisions, how the safeguards work, and what the AI has learned from Brent's weapons.

## Purpose and reasoning control plane

v2.10 uses four stable documents:

- `docs/Project-North-Star.md` — what success means and what the project optimizes for
- `docs/Brent-Decision-Doctrine.md` — how deeply and skeptically the AI should reason
- `docs/Brent-Personal-Evidence-Ledger.md` — what Brent actually said and what is current
- `docs/State-Reconciliation-Protocol.md` — how corrections propagate without stale assumptions

Together they prevent five recurring errors:

- Applying equal thought to trivial and high-impact decisions.
- Treating an old bootstrap as more authoritative than a newer Brent statement.
- Turning an assistant inference into something Brent supposedly said.
- Updating one sentence without checking affected analyses, prompts, or recommendations.
- Reasoning carefully toward the wrong objective, such as maximum retention or empty slots instead of stronger builds.

## Project priorities and safety rules

- Primary success metric: stronger and more enjoyable builds for desired content.
- Secondary metric: inventory clarity and reduced decision fatigue.
- Distant tertiary metric: vault space.
- Begin broad analysis from current build engines, especially Warlock, and produce a proactive upgrade plan.

- Never dismantle directly from an AI ranking.
- Back up DIM metadata before imports.
- Preserve user-created tags, notes, locks, favorites, keeps, loadouts, and meaningful personal signals.
- Compare exact versions, frames, firing behavior, elements, origin traits, legal Tier configurations, and complete configured stat packages.
- Current Brent statements govern player fit; the newest DIM export governs current inventory; verified mechanics govern game behavior.
- Require an auditable retained replacement for every proposed deletion.
- For consequential deletions, include the strongest keep argument, likely regret cause, and what would reverse the conclusion.
