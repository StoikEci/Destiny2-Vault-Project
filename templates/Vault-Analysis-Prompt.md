# Vault Analysis Prompt

> Analyze my newest DIM export as a complete loadout toolbox. First read the authoritative AI bootstrap, Project North Star, Decision Doctrine, Personal Evidence Ledger, and State Reconciliation Protocol. Treat current direct player statements as authoritative for player fit, the newest DIM export as authoritative for current inventory, and verified mechanics as authoritative for game behavior. Never promote an AI inference or earlier assistant assertion into a player fact.
>
> The primary objective is stronger, more enjoyable, more capable loadouts; inventory clarity is secondary and space is tertiary. Begin broad work from current Warlock engines while preserving meaningful portable and other-class value. Allocate analysis depth according to irreversibility, personal significance, reacquisition cost, coverage consequence, Tier/configuration complexity, evidence conflict, and policy impact. Use current official mechanics, dated creator evidence, Community Insights, community-supported god rolls, and coherent real-role perk synergy. Wishlist absence, zero kills, unlocked status, missing tags, and lack of loadout use are neutral; positive metadata may protect or break ties.
>
> Enumerate all useful legal Tier configurations and compare exact weapon versions, intrinsic frames, firing behavior, elements, slots/ammo, origin traits, Champion roles, source/reacquisition cost, and complete configured stat packages. Evaluate mechanical, portable, class/build-adjusted, rotation, and player-fit value separately before reconciling them. For Hunter, distinguish Marksman's Dodge from passive holstered reload, magazine overflow, sustained-fire refunds, ammo economy, and reload-triggered payoff perks.
>
> For every serious item, answer “What new or stronger loadout does keeping/deleting this enable?” For every serious Junk candidate, provide a decision receipt: candidate, retained replacement, defined roles, exact coverage, lost tradeoff, current mechanic/community basis, relevant Personal Evidence Ledger ID(s), source cost, strongest honest keep argument, likely regret cause, confidence, and what new evidence would reverse the conclusion. Never overwrite user-created metadata or use a different frame/version as a silent replacement. If one material unknown remains, identify the highest-value next check and use Manual Review rather than manufacturing certainty.


> Finish every major analysis with a prioritized Armory Improvement Plan: highest-impact build gap, recommended chase/craft/focus/enhancement/catalyst/test, loadout or content unlocked, current item replaced or demoted, source effort, and urgency. Be willing to delete clearly outclassed rolls when a superior option covers the engines and roles the player actually uses; do not let theoretical coverage or protection rules become automatic retention.
